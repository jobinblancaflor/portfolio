# Portfolio Judge Rubric

Score each as PASS/FAIL. All 9 must PASS for `OVERALL: PASS`. These are
the things a human hiring manager notices in the first 10 seconds and the
first minute — not things `gate.py` can grep for.

1. **First-screen clarity** — without scrolling, a stranger can tell in
   under 5 seconds who this is and what they do (not just a name/logo).
2. **Visual hierarchy** — heading sizes, spacing, and layout guide the
   eye in a clear order (name/positioning → proof → contact), not a flat
   wall of same-sized text.
3. **Whitespace / breathing room** — sections aren't cramped; margins and
   padding look deliberate, not default-browser-stylesheet.
4. **Typography** — a font choice beyond the plain system default, with
   consistent sizing/weight scale (not 6 different random sizes).
5. **Color use** — a coherent, limited palette (not default blue links on
   white with no accent, and not a rainbow of unrelated colors).
6. **Project case study depth** — each of the 5 projects reads as
   problem → approach → outcome (even if placeholder-flavored), not just
   a name + one adjective.
7. **Copy tone** — professional and confident; free of obvious
   lorem-ipsum, "Lorem ipsum dolor," placeholder Latin, or template
   boilerplate phrases like "Welcome to my website."
8. **Consistency** — spacing/color/type choices repeat predictably across
   sections; it doesn't look stitched together from different templates.
9. **Send-to-a-hiring-manager test** — taken as a whole, would this read
   as a real candidate's site, not an obviously AI-scaffolded placeholder
   page (setting aside that the specific project details are known
   placeholders per GOAL.md).
